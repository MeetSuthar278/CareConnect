# Project overview

## Problem

People who need community support may not know which organization can help. Organizations may also have limited capacity or may not respond in time.

## Solution

CareConnect connects members with approved community organizations. Members select a service, area and preferred time frame. The system finds an eligible organization and sends a referral. If the organization rejects the referral or does not respond in time, the system looks for a backup. Coordinators help when automatic matching cannot resolve a request.

## Sustainable Development Goals

**SDG 17 – Partnerships for the Goals (primary):** CareConnect improves cooperation between community organizations through shared referrals, backup matching and request tracking.

**SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-Being (secondary):** The system helps people access non-medical support such as food assistance, peer support and seniors' activities.

## Intended users

Members request support, organization representatives provide services, and coordinators manage approvals and unresolved requests. The project is an academic demonstration, not an emergency service.
